### 🎯 About Me

- 🎯 I build the platform layer under AI: data pipelines, model serving, observability and distributed storage, so ML and LLM systems run reliably in production.
- 🏫 Master of Information Technology (AI) at [The University of Western Australia](https://www.uwa.edu.au/), a top 100 university in [QS Ranking](https://www.topuniversities.com/universities/university-western-australia). B.Eng. in Electronics and Telecommunications at [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/), where my undergraduate work covered AI for anomaly detection and speech processing in agriculture.
- 🏢 1+ year as a Backend Developer (C#, SQL Server, Python), now focused on distributed systems, data engineering and MLOps.
- 🔭 Currently building: an LSM-tree compaction engine in Go (seqcomp), a speech-to-text MLOps platform, and a RAG system deployed with Docker and Ansible.
- 🤝 Looking for AI/ML platform, data infrastructure or MLOps roles, and open-source or non-profit work in distributed systems.
